# Authors

## Daniel T Sasser II

Daniel T Sasser II is the creator and lead architect of SIM-ONE Alpha, the
founder and CEO of [Gorombo](https://gorombo.com), and the creator of the
[SIM-ONE Framework](https://simoneframework.org).

Daniel is a software and systems design engineer, independent AI researcher,
and cognitive architect with more than 25 years of experience across protocol
systems, network engineering, web platforms, automation, and AI architecture.
His work focuses on governance-first AI: placing protocols, oversight,
validation, context, permissions, and human authority in the system
architecture instead of relying on model behavior alone.

For SIM-ONE Alpha, Daniel:

- created the product concept and governance architecture;
- defined how the SIM-ONE Framework is applied to an orchestrating AI employee;
- leads the product's architecture, development, and release direction through
  Gorombo.

## Project Ownership

SIM-ONE Alpha is developed and maintained by Gorombo as the base architecture
behind Gorombo's AI Employees. It is a self-hosted product that applies the
SIM-ONE Framework's protocol-governed approach on
[Flue](https://flueframework.com/), the TypeScript agent runtime from the Astro
ecosystem.

These names identify separate parts of the project:

- **Gorombo** is the company that develops and maintains SIM-ONE Alpha.
- **SIM-ONE Alpha** is the AI employee product in this repository.
- **The SIM-ONE Framework** is the governance architecture applied by the
  product.
- **Flue** is the underlying agent runtime.
